Privacy Policy

App: “Auntie's Random” (com.paauan.auntie) · Last updated: July 3, 2026

“Auntie's Random” is an entertainment randomizer / decision-making app. We care about your privacy and collect as little data as possible. This document explains how the app handles your data.

1. Data the app stores

  • Data kept only on your device: your food menus, the names you type, and your membership status are saved in your device's local storage only. They are never sent to our servers (we do not operate any server that stores user data).
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob to support the developer. Google's advertising system may collect and use data such as your Advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use our services

Subscribers of “Love Auntie (Pro)” will see no ads, and the app will not call the ad system.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The app offers a paid subscription (Pro) that removes ads. All payments are processed through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in the Google Play Store app.

4. Children

This app is intended for a general audience. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the “Last updated” date above.
