Auntie's Random 👵🎲

Can't decide? Let Auntie decide for you.

Auntie's Random is a fun, fast decision-maker app packed with more than 20 randomizers — from "What to Eat" and "Decision Dice" to horoscopes, fortune sticks, team splitters, workout roulette, and more. Every result comes with a warm (and slightly sassy) comment from Auntie herself.

  • 🍜 What to Eat — spin a wheel of your own food choices
  • 🎲 Decision Dice, Yes/No, Heads or Tails
  • 🔮 Daily Horoscope, Fortune Sticks, Daily Wisdom
  • 👕 Outfit Picker, Travel Picker
  • 🎉 Lucky Draw, Team Split, Queue Order, Who's It?
  • 💪 Workout Roulette, Clean Eats, Study Mission, Break Time
  • 🎡 My Wheel — build your own custom randomizer (Pro)

Share any result as a picture with friends, and unlock "Love Auntie (Pro)" to remove ads and unlock My Wheel for free.
